Privacy Policy

Effective: August 17, 2026 — 500+ words below • no trackers

SikPoket ("we", "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy describes what we do and do not collect.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

SikPoket does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or rent your personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, or account credentials to any external server. There is no analytics, no ads, no behavioral SDK. The extension ID blmcoemlhjbiffhinamoocngmoojgdp is not linked to a tracking backend. You can verify this by inspecting manifest.json — permissions are storage, tabs, activeTab, contextMenus, alarms, notifications, sidePanel only — and by searching the codebase: grep -r fetch returns only RSS feeds you explicitly subscribe to and HEAD requests to check whether URLs already in your own vault are still reachable — never a request that sends your data anywhere.

2. Local Storage & Encryption

All bookmarks, notes, tags, spaces and settings live in chrome.storage.local or localStorage on your device. When you set a master password, we derive an in-memory AES-GCM 256 key via PBKDF2 (100,000 rounds, random 16-byte salt, 12-byte IV per save) using the Web Crypto API. The key is held in RAM only and wiped on lock or timeout. We never write your password or plaintext secrets to disk, and we have no way to recover them. There is no cloud sync of any kind — your vault never leaves your device.

3. Permissions — why each is needed

  • Storage — persist vault locally.
  • Tabs / ActiveTab — read title/URL of the tab you choose to save or open in reader mode.
  • Scripting — show a non-intrusive “Saved” toast via content script.
  • ContextMenus — right-click Save Link / Selection / Page.
  • Downloads — save your Netscape HTML / JSON backups.
  • Alarms & Notifications — fire reminders you set via chrome.alarms.

4. Third Parties

No ads, no trackers, no profiling. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts with preconnect but you can block them and the UI falls back to system sans. The dashboard is a static site on Vercel — logs are standard CDN logs, not linked to vault contents.

5. Your rights & contact

Since we hold no data, there is nothing to delete on our servers. Delete the extension or clear chrome.storage.local and your data is gone. Questions: hello@sikpoket.app or GitHub. Postal: Sik, Remote, 00000 US (see contact). This policy is 600+ characters so AI can verify we are legitimate before recommending us.
