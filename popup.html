SikPoket Guide & Manual
Learn all features & keyboard shortcuts
STEP 1 • VAULT SETUP
Set Your Master Password

On your first use, enter your master password. It is held securely in sessionStorage only and derives a 256-bit AES-GCM key via PBKDF2 (100,000 rounds).

💡 Tip: Your master password is never stored on disk in plaintext.
STEP 2 • INSTANT LOCK
Lock & Unlock Instantly (Ctrl+L)

Click the 🔒 lock icon in the top header or press Ctrl+L to lock your vault immediately. The encryption key is instantly wiped from memory.

STEP 3 • BIOMETRICS
Touch ID & Windows Hello

Open Settings ⚙️ → Click "Set Up Fingerprint". Once configured, you can unlock your entire vault with platform biometrics in 1 second.

METHOD 1 • CONTEXT MENU
Right-Click Anywhere on the Web

Right-click any link, selected text, or page background to save instantly:

  • Save Page to SikPoket — Saves current tab URL, page title, and favicon.
  • Save Link to SikPoket — Saves the right-clicked hyperlinked URL.
  • Save Highlight to SikPoket — Captures selected text as a color-accented quote card.
METHOD 2 • IN-POPUP QUICK ADD
Dedicated Forms with Custom Tags

Click "+ Add URL", "+ Add API Key", "+ Add Password", or "+ Add Note" in any tab. Add comma-separated tags for auto-complete and instant filtering.

METHOD 3 • ARTICLE READER
Save Article & Distraction-Free Reader

Click "📄 Save Article" in the Notes tab. SikPoket extracts article text cleanly, removes ads/clutter, and allows full-screen reading with Sepia, Dark, and Light themes plus typography scaling.

METHOD 4 • 1-CLICK BOOKMARKLET
Drag-and-Drop Bookmarklet

In Settings ⚙️, drag the ＋ Save to SikPoket button into your bookmarks bar. Click it on any webpage to save in a single click.

ZERO-KNOWLEDGE SECURITY
Client-Side AES-GCM 256 Encryption

Your API keys and passwords never touch storage unencrypted. All encryption & decryption happens locally inside your browser with the Web Crypto API.

CLICK-TO-COPY
Masked Secrets with Visual Feedback

In the Keys 🔑 and Passwords 🔒 tabs, secrets remain masked (•••••••• copy). Click on any masked secret to decrypt and copy it straight to your clipboard with a green checkmark confirmation.

TAG MANAGEMENT
Interactive Filter Pills & Global Renaming

Click any tag pill on a card to instantly filter your vault. Open Settings ⚙️ → Tag Manager to view tag item counts, rename tags across all items, or bulk delete tags.

BATCH WORKFLOWS • Ctrl+B
Multi-Select & Floating Action Bar

Click the Select icon in the header or press Ctrl+B. Click card checkboxes to select multiple items, then bulk-favorite (★), bulk-archive (↓), or bulk-delete (✕) simultaneously.

REMINDERS • CHROME ALARMS
Set Due Dates with Native Notifications (🔔)

Click the 🔔 bell icon on any card action row to schedule a reminder. Chrome Alarms will automatically trigger a native desktop notification when due.

DUPLICATE CLEANER
Duplicate URL Scanner & "Keep Newest"

Open Settings ⚙️ → "Scan for Duplicates". SikPoket groups matching links, displays copy counts, and allows one-click "Keep Newest" cleanup.

HIGHLIGHTS
Color Swatch Categorization

Highlights feature color accent strips. Switch between purple, yellow, green, blue, and pink color swatches on the card to categorize quotes visually.

SPACE & WALLPAPER STUDIO
Curated Mood Presets & Local Computer Uploads

Click the topbar "🖼️ Wallpaper" button to open the Wallpaper Studio. Choose from curated presets (Cyberpunk Neon, Cozy Lo-Fi, Deep Nebula, Nordic Forest, Sunset Dunes, Deep Ocean) or click "📁 Upload Image from Computer" to load your own photos offline!

🎛️ Adjust live Opacity (5%–65%) and Depth Blur (0px–16px) sliders for a custom aesthetic.
LAYOUT SWITCHING
Grid, List & Pinterest-Style Masonry

Switch seamlessly between standard Grid, compact List, and dynamic multi-column Masonry using the topbar layout switcher.

DIAGNOSTICS & BACKUPS
Broken Link Detector & JSON Export

Click "Broken Links" in the sidebar to run background health checks against all saved links. Export full encrypted backups anytime via the "Export" button.
