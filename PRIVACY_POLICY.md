# Privacy Policy for SikPoket

**Effective Date:** August 17, 2026

SikPoket ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding information collection, usage, and disclosure.

## 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
SikPoket does **not** collect, store, transmit, sell, or rent your personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, or account credentials to any external servers or third parties.

## 2. Local Data Storage & Encryption
- All bookmarks, notes, tags, space groupings, and user configurations are stored locally on your device using Chrome's extension storage API (`chrome.storage.local`) or your browser's `localStorage`.
- Any items saved with encryption are secured using client-side cryptographic primitives (AES-GCM / SHA-256). We have no access to your master password or decrypted data.

## 3. Extension Permissions
SikPoket requests permissions solely for the functionality of its features:
- **Storage:** To persist your bookmarks and settings locally on your machine.
- **Tabs & ActiveTab:** To read the title and URL of the current tab when you choose to save it or launch reader mode.
- **Scripting:** To display a non-intrusive save confirmation notification on the active webpage.
- **Context Menus:** To provide right-click options for saving links, images, or selections.
- **Downloads:** To save your backup files (Netscape HTML / JSON) to your local disk when you click export.
- **Alarms & Notifications:** To trigger reminders you explicitly set for specific bookmarks.

## 4. Third-Party Services
SikPoket does not embed third-party advertising, trackers, analytics, or behavioral profiling SDKs.
